# Locked evaluation criteria

Prepared before this evaluation run. These cases have not been used to adjust prompts or the 0.95 issue-grouping gate. Preserve earlier evaluation files. Labels are author expectations, not customer outcomes.

Growth: compare Gemini qualification and grounded drafts against the existing conservative source gate and a fixed short capability answer. Use identical source, current verified claims, destination, 280-character limit, and no posting in either arm. The fixed baseline uses zero model calls; record that cost advantage. Reject any false contact eligibility, unsupported capability claim, missing destination or overlength draft. Target at least 80% recall of the eight explicit, supported requests. If this fails, retain assisted Product review and narrow discovery; do not tune on these same held-out cases. Blind human review of usefulness and edit effort remains separate from structural checks. No uptake or revenue claim follows from synthetic sources.

Grouping: compare actual Gemini classifier plus unchanged 0.95 gate against a conservative explicit keyword baseline on 20 new reports. Record raw match, confidence, gated result, false merges and missed merges. Zero false merges is required for safe automatic grouping; target 80% recall of same-mechanism reports. If recall is lower, keep operator link/review fallback and abstention. Never lower the gate to pass this set. Historical calendar misses remain evidence regardless of this result.

Voice: three novel diagnostic turns through the public deployed service, with real Cloud synthesis/transcription, one case, replay deduplication and no repeated known questions or false resolution. Synthetic input proves service integration, not microphone capture or perceived naturalness. Human listening remains required.

A later opt-in activation experiment needs at least 20 eligible visitors per arm, matched distribution budget and seven-day observation. This is deferred validation until participants exist, not authorization to recruit strangers or invent results.

Draft follow-up: lock twenty additional supported inquiries before new generation. This evaluates wording/usefulness, separately from mixed-source eligibility. Keep the current verified facts and the same full opaque campaign destination/280-character budget in both arms. Model arm receives one call per inquiry; fixed baseline receives none. Randomize A/B presentation and keep the answer key private until human review. Preserve every rejected generation; no retry to select a passing answer. Require zero unsupported claims, at least sixteen replies needing no substantive factual correction and median human review under two minutes. A structural pass does not substitute for blind usefulness/edit/time review. These are synthetic inquiries, not live provider sources or acquisition records.
