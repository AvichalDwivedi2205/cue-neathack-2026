02 — Growth

The fix becomes the pitch.

Growth finds people who are explicitly asking for something Bloom does, checks that the capability is verified, and drafts a reply. People handle everything after that.

  • Explicit asks only. Complaints about other apps and vague posts are dismissed.
  • Verified capabilities only. No claim goes out without a passing acceptance check.
  • Reviewed, then posted by a person. Approval isn’t publication. You record the live URL.
  • Honest results. Controlled test traffic never counts toward the funnel.

03 — Agents

Four agents. Each knows its limits.

Every agent is a named role with a purpose, a list of what it can and can’t do, and its own health status. Ask it anything and it will tell you what it’s doing, and what it won’t do.

  • Questions don’t start jobs. A status check stays a status check.
  • No duplicate work. Repeated instructions attach to the task already running.
  • Chat can’t approve. Approvals happen in review, on the exact commit or draft.
  • Health ≠ progress. A connected agent can still have a failing task, and an idle one never looks busy.

06 — Questions

Straight answers.

Does Cue deploy anything on its own?

No. Agents propose changes, and a person approves the exact commit. Approval deploys to demo, and the issue resolves only after the customer’s scenario passes there.

What happens when three people report the same bug?

The reports join one issue and share one investigation. Each customer still gets their own reply, in the channel they used.

Will Growth post replies for us?

No. Growth drafts replies that cite only verified capabilities. A person reviews the draft, another person posts it from the brand account, and the live URL is recorded.

Can I talk to the agents from Slack?

Yes, for example @Cue engineering status ISS-14. Questions never start work, repeated instructions attach to the existing task, and approvals stay in review controls.

Which channels does it connect to?

The in-app widget, email, X (operator capture and mention collection), Reddit notifications, Slack, GitHub and the Codex coding agent.

How do I know an agent is actually working?

Service health and task state are shown separately. Every task has a state, an owner, a next step and a full event history in Agent activity.
